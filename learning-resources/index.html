How to Import the Decks

Once you have your decks, open Anki and click "Import File" (or go to File > Import...) to import your decks OR just drag and drop them into the Anki window.

That's it, you're done! Now you're ready to drill the vocab or kanji for Kantan Japanese!

How to Use the Spreadsheets

The spreadsheets for our vocabulary and kanji can be used with:

  • Libre Office (free and open source)
  • Google Sheets (free, requires account and online connection)
  • Microsoft Excel (paid)

and any other spreadsheet software that is not listed here.

Using the Spreadsheets with Google Sheets

For Google Sheets, the file needs to be converted for use with Google Sheets. To do this:

  1. Go to Google Drive.
  2. Drag and drop the spreadsheet file into your drive.
  3. Double click the uploaded sheet to open it in Google Sheets.
Once inside, you can view the contents, edit them, etc.
