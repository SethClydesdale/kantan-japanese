{WORD}{BUTTONS} {MEANING} When counting from 1-10, 4 can be pronounced as よん or し. し is sometimes avoided for superstition reasons because it has the same pronunciation as 死し (death). This is the reason why 4 is considered an "unlucky number" in Japan. Certain counters may call for only one pronunciation or shorten it, however. For example, when よん is used with ～時じji (...o'clock) the んn is dropped, making it 四時よじyo ji (four o'clock) and the same goes for 四人よにんyo nin (four people).{TAGS}

{WORD}{BUTTONS} {MEANING} When counting from 1-10, 7 can be pronounced as なな or しち. Much like with し4, しち is sometimes avoided because it contains 死し (death) in the pronunciation. Unlike 4, however, 7 is considered a lucky number. Additionally, only one variant may be used depending on what pronunciation a counter calls for. For example, 七時しちじshichi ji (seven o'clock), 七年ななねんnana nen (7 years), etc.{TAGS}

{WORD}{BUTTONS} {MEANING} When counting from 1-10, 9 can be pronounced as きゅう or く. く is sometimes avoided because it sounds similar to 苦く (pain/suffering). Because of this, 9 is considered an unlucky number. You'll see く used with counters, however, such as 九時くじku ji (nine o'clock), 九月くがつku gatsu (September), and so on.{TAGS}
{WORD}{BUTTONS} {MEANING} After じゅう, the pronunciation of numbers repeats. The previous numbers you learned will either be appended (じゅういち11、じゅうに2…) or prepended (にじゅう20、さんじゅう30…) onto じゅう or both (ろくじゅうなな67) to make newer and bigger numbers.{TAGS}

{WORD}{BUTTONS} {MEANING} ひゃく is where things finally change! Just like with じゅう, you append or prepend the numbers you learned earlier to make bigger numbers. For example, にひゃくni hyaku is 200, さんびゃくごじゅうsan byaku go juu is 350, and so on. An important thing to know about ひゃく is that the beginning pronunciation changes depending on the number that comes before it:
300 さんびゃくsan byaku (h changes to b)
600 ろっぴゃくroppyaku (h changes to pp, く is dropped on ろく)
800 はっぴゃくhappyaku (h changes to pp, ち is dropped on はち)
Fortunately these are the only three exceptions that you need to memorize for this number. {TAGS}
