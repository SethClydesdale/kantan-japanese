Use the chart below to learn how each Katakana character is pronounced. To help memorize each character, the following method is recommended:

  1. Study 5-10 characters a a time. (e.g., アa-オo, カka-コko, サsa-ソso, etc.)
  2. After studying, try matching all of the Katakana you studied to their respective Romaji by doing the corresponding practice exercise for each chart.
  3. Take a break for 5-10 mintues or longer.
  4. Try matching all the characters you learned again.
  5. If you successfully get them all, awesome! You can learn more Katakana or take a break for the day. If not, take a short break (e.g., 5 mintues) and then try reviewing them again.
  6. Repeat Steps 1-5 each day until you've memorized the entire chart. Then, move onto the next chart.

During steps 2 and 4 it is recommended to try matching ALL of the Katakana you've learned; not just the 5 or 10 you're currently studying. See our Learn Katakana guide for more tips on learning the Katakana.
