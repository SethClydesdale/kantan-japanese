The Basic Numbers

To begin, let's take a look at how numbers 0-10 plus 100 are pronounced in Japanese.

Numeral Hiragana Audio
0 ゼロzero/れいrei rei
1 いちichi ichi
2 にni ni
3 さんsan san
4 よんyon/しshi yon
5 ごgo go
6 ろくroku roku
7 ななnana/しちshichi nana
8 はちhachi hachi
9 きゅうkyuu/くku kyuu
10 じゅうじゅう juu
100 ひゃくhyaku hyaku

As you can see above, some numbers have multiple pronunciations. The pronunciation used depends on a number of factors, such as the counter used with the number and even personal preference when counting. The most common pronunciation used in counting has been placed first, so just focus on memorizing these for now until you're ready to tie another pronunciation to the same numeral.
