Vocabulary Dictionary

Welcome to the Vocabulary Dictionary! This page contains words from Kantan Japanese's courses and various popular textbooks. It also allows you to practice a selected list of words in the exercise of your choice, so you can learn and memorize new words or focus on the ones you're having trouble with.

Practice練習する

To begin practicing, select 5 or more words by clicking the checkbox next to any word to add it to your study queue. You can also use the select all buttons to select a group of words. Once you have selected all the words you want, click the button below to begin.
