This chart shows how sounds in numbers (1-10) and counters change according to their combination. You can review this chart to better familiarize yourself with how numbers and counters behave. For example, look at the sound change, then an example counter at the bottom which shows how the sound changes for that counter when it begins with a specific letter, such as "k": いち  いっ + ～階かい = 一階いっかい.

Notes

  1. Hiragana indicate the sound changes in numbers, and letters show the changes in the initial consonant of counters.
  2. ( ) means that the change is optional.
  3. An empty box means no sound change occurs.
