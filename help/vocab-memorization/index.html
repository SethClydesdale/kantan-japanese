Vocabulary Memorization Tips

Seth
5/17/20223/20/2026

The following tips are for use with the vocab exercises on this website and should help you with memorizing the vocab for each lesson.

  • After completing a vocab exercise, take a break, as this will help with reinforcing your memory. I recommend taking incrementally longer break periods between exercises. Starting at 5min breaks and increasing by 5min or 10min each time until you hit your personal limit, such as 60min, or until you feel you've memorized the vocab. After this you can take a break for the day and revisit the exercise tomorrow to see how well you remember the vocab.
  • Try to do vocabulary reviews when they appear in your review list. If you have SRS enabled, the vocabulary exercises you've done will show up in the Reviews page, so you won't need to keep track yourself.
  • If you're having trouble memorizing readings for Kanji, keep the furigana visible for at least the first 3 exercise sessions, then take it away and only display it when you cannot remember the reading AT ALL.
  • Limit yourself to a set amount of words per day. Personally, I feel around 15-30 words to be the sweet spot, but everyone is different, so take it a little at a time and don't overwhelm yourself or rush.
  • Find material to read that utilizes the vocab you're studying to help further aid with memorization. For example, you can find example sentences by using Tatoeba or jisho.org, or even websites such as twitter to see native examples. Adding "filter:images" to a search on twitter also helps a ton with visualization. You can also purchase children's books in Japanese, or beginner manga such as Yotsuba&!

I hope that these tips will aid you with your vocab studies!

As a final note, if you have trouble with this method, I would highly recommend trying Anki instead with our Anki decks to see if you like Anki better.
