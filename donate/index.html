Donations and Sponsorships

If you're a student or someone struggling to make ends meet, please don't worry about donating and consider saving the money for your own expenses. The thought or your vocal support are more than enough!

Kantan Japanese was created by Seth Clydesdale, with the goal to make learning and practicing the Japanese language easier, for everyone. This project is Open Source and 100% free, which means that I primarily rely on donations and sponorships to support myself and our development. If you found this project helpful and would like to support my work, please see below for the different ways that you can contribute.

  • Support us on Patreon.
  • Sponsor me on GitHub.
  • Buy me a coffee via Ko-fi.
  • Send me a tip via PayPal.

As thanks for supporting the development of Kantan Japanese, we'll add your name and avatar to the sponsors list at the bottom of this page. If supporting directly through PayPal, however, we'll keep your donation private unless otherwise stated, to preserve your privacy.

You can also support my work in other ways.

  • Star the project on GitHub; Stars show appreciation for a repository maintainer's work and helps increase the visibility of the project.
  • Spread the word about Kantan Japanese; let people know how it helped you or how it can help them with their studies!
  • Feel free to contact me via one of the platforms located under "Links/Contact" if you'd like to get in contact or just say thanks.

I greatly appreciate your support, as it helps motivate me to continue working on and improving my projects. Thank you!
