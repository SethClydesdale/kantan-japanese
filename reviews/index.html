About Our Spaced Repetition System

Our Spaced Repetition System uses a modified version of the SM-2 algorithm that Anki uses. It functions in the following manner:

  • Exercises done for the first time or reviews that have been failed (less than 60% score) are reviewed the next day.
  • Once a review date has been set, it will not change until the day of the review. So, if you want to retry an exercise immediately, you can do so without worrying about your next review date being altered.
  • For exercises that you don't want to review, click the "moon" button next to it to retire that exercise from any future reviews. Should you want to revive that exercise for review later on, you can retrieve it from the "retired" tab in the review list.
  • During reviews, the next review date is determined based on your score and problems solved. An additional "streak" parameter determines when the exercise is considered learned or "mastered."
    • A score of 90-100% or only getting 1 problem wrong with a score of 75% or higher increases the streak by 1.
    • A score of 80-89% decreases the streak by 1.
    • A score of 70-79% decreases the streak by 2.
    • A score of 60-69% decreases the streak by 3.
    • A score less than 60% resets the streak and forces a next day review.
  • Due reviews can be done whenever you like and do not need to be completed right away to maintain the streak. However, it is recommended to do them as soon as possible during early phases (e.g., Streak 0-2) to prevent forgetting newly learned information. In short, take it easy and only review when you have the time; don't force yourself.
The higher your streak, the further out reviews will be. Reviews typically progress in the following manner should you consistenly pass with a score of 90% or higher.
  • Streak 0: 1 day
  • Streak 1: 6 days
  • Streak 2: 15 days
  • Streak 3: 38 days
  • Streak 4: 94 days
  • Streak 5: mastered; no longer reviewed
Please note that for Streak 1 or higher the days listed are approximate. We apply a bit of fuzz to ensure that reviews do not all end up on the same day should you review a large amount of exercises in one day. In addition to that, your score also plays a small role in determining how far out reviews are set, with reviews being further out for higher scores and much sooner for lower scores.
